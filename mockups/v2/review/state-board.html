SYNTHETIC PLACEHOLDER — NOT SOURCE OR MODEL OUTPUT
StoryAtlas mockup v2 · deliverable 11
State board

Every state the brief requires, with the mark that means it and — the part that matters — the state it must never be confused with. Each card's third line is the confusion this encoding exists to prevent.

Twenty-nine cards across four sections. Ten of them would collapse into one “grey box” in an ordinary dashboard. The single rule behind all of them: an absent mark and a zero mark are different scientific claims, and so are the eight ways of being absent.

A · Value states

Anywhere a quantity could appear: a feature track, a matrix cell, a coverage gauge, an inspector field.

Present

A compiled value. Magnitude is carried by area or height, never by tint alone.

Not “good”. A present value can still be low, uncertain, or thinly supported.

Zero

A measured zero. The baseline rule is drawn at the axis so the reader sees a value, not an absence.

Not missing. Something was measured and it came out zero.

Missing

The provider was asked and returned nothing over this extent. Hatched, and excluded from every denominator.

Not zero, and never interpolated across. Bridging the gap would invent data.

Not requested

This quantity was never asked for in the current view specification. One click requests it.

Not unavailable. Nothing has failed; nothing has been tried.

Unauthorised

The value exists and this viewer may not resolve it. Structure and receipts stay visible.

Not missing. Withholding is a policy decision and is labelled as one.

Low coverage

A real value over thin support. The stipple density is the coverage fraction, and the number travels with it.

Not a low value. Coverage and magnitude are different axes.

Unresolved

The compiler reached no decision and is saying so. Candidates remain listed in the inspector.

Not an average of the candidates. Splitting the difference manufactures a claim.

Off-source

Mass that reaches a destination outside the source. Five named kinds, never one bin.

Not an error, and not “irrelevant”. Some off-source mass is the finding.

Unranked

Held mass. Nothing was ranked, the total still reaches one, and the abstention is auditable.

Not empty. An unranked unit and an unassessed unit are different records.

Stale build

The receipt on this value no longer matches the compiled model. Struck through until reconciled.

Not merely old. A stale value may be wrong, so it is not offered as a reading.

B · Selection placement and evidence horizon

Selection is never dropped. When it cannot be drawn, that fact is itself drawn.

on-mark

On mark

The selected address has a direct visual mark in this projection. The 3px 6/3 dash is the Atlas selection stroke already shipping.

Not “visible”. A mark can be occluded and still be on-mark; it stays keyboard-reachable.

via-ancestor

Via ancestor

The object has no mark at this level; its containing scene or episode stands in, and names what it is standing in for.

Not a coarser selection. The semantic address is unchanged; only the drawing is.

off-projection

Off projection

Still selected, outside what this projection or filter can show. The chip persists in the focus bar with a route back.

Not deselected, and not an error. Changing projection must never clear focus.

Omniscient

No horizon. Every compiled claim is admissible. The readout says so in words: omniscient (no horizon; 584 code units).

Not “the reader has finished”. The slider sits at the end, but the two states are distinct and the receipt records which.

Reader at a point

Material after the horizon is struck through, not merely faded, and the identity capacity of both compilers drops with it.

Not dimming for emphasis. Fade alone would be ambiguous with missing, external, and unimportant.

C · Recall and alignment states

The five off-source destinations are scored separately, and abstention is held apart from all of them, so no card here says “external”.

Multimodal alignment

Mass genuinely divided between two source targets. Both squares are drawn at their true area; neither is promoted.

Not a tie to be broken. Forcing a winner would delete the finding.

Distortion facet

Right address, wrong content. Facets — role, polarity, location, attribute, reported-as-fact — are flagged beside the cell, never inside its mass.

Not a lower posterior. Address correctness and fidelity are orthogonal axes.

Off-source, named · 1 of 2

An association reaches outside the source; commentary is talk about the task; an intrusion asserts content the source does not license.

Not one bin. These three have different scientific consequences and different follow-up actions.

Off-source, named · 2 of 2

An inference the source supports but never states, and material no coder could resolve. Both are off-source; only one is an error.

Not the same as intrusion. An inference is licensed; an intrusion is not.

Unranked / abstained

The unit was not ranked against any destination. Its mass is held in its own column and counted in the row total.

Not zero, and not an empty row. Abstention is a measurement, and it keeps the row summing to one.

Source omission

A source target received no aggregate mass. It is a column property, drawn on the source axis with its denominator declared.

Never a [skipped] token in the recall rail. Nothing is inserted into participant speech.

Misordering

An inversion between two consecutive source-addressed units. It lives on the edge between cells, with a stated denominator.

Never a cell class. A single cell cannot be out of order; only a transition can.

Privacy-blocked

Identity matched, resolution not authorised. Structure, mass and receipts remain inspectable; the words do not appear.

Not missing data. The value exists and is being withheld — a separate gate from identity.

D · Packet, build and layout stress

The states that decide whether the workspace can be trusted at all.

compiling · no partial claims shown

Loading and compilation

Structure is reserved; no value, mark or label is drawn until its packet compiles. Asynchronous results are last-intent-wins.

Never a placeholder number that later changes. A shown value is a compiled value.

— no data —

No data

The packet compiled and contains nothing for this view. An explicit statement, in words, in the place the content would have been.

Not an empty panel. Silence is indistinguishable from a rendering failure.

No coherent episode model accepted

No accepted episode model

An autobiographical interview whose details do not support any episode structure. Details, addresses and phenomenology all remain fully usable.

Not a failure. It is a finding, and it stays selectable beside the alternatives.

Build receipt does not match view 8492e43 · model a91c04e

Stale or incompatible receipt

The saved view and the compiled model disagree. Both fingerprints are printed; the view is struck through until it is recompiled or explicitly accepted.

Not a silent re-render against the new build. That would fabricate a result nobody computed.

Long labels and overlapping annotations

Labels truncate by a stable priority; colliding marks bundle into a counted chip. The bundle is a layout-only aggregation — it retains every member id and a deterministic descent path to each.

Never a dropped mark. A mark removed to make the picture tidy is a deleted observation.

Very long work, dense overview

Beyond the mark budget the overview shows density and boundaries only — never a hairball of individual marks. The viewport rectangle and the selection stay legible because nothing competes with them.

Not a zoomed-out version of the detail view. It answers “where am I”, not “what is here”.
